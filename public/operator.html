Entry and exit gates

Mark where vehicles enter and where they leave. When you generate the layout, MARGA plans the driving lanes and numbers the slots around these gates: slots closest to the gates come first, accessible and EV-charging slots are placed nearest the gates, and every aisle connects the entry to the exit. The gates are drawn on the layout below as you move them.

After saving, generate the layout again (below) so the slots are planned around the gates.

Digital layout generator

Enter the site’s measured dimensions. Marga proposes numbered virtual slots using vehicle size, driving aisles and a 1 m safety margin. Review, drag slots to adjust, block any you don’t want, then publish. The layout is only a proposed arrangement – it must be calibrated to the real site and does not certify the land as legally or physically suitable for parking.
